# Free-tier-first deployment policy

- Do not assume any provider's free plan has unlimited bandwidth, compute, storage, or concurrent media capacity.
- Keep AI outside the media path and disable expensive features by default.
- Use client-side rendering for preview/avatars when practical.
- Measure egress, recording storage, room duration, and compute before launch.
- 10 rooms × 4 participants is a future load-test target, not a current capacity claim.
- Before commercial launch, document provider limits, data retention, recovery, and cost ceilings.
